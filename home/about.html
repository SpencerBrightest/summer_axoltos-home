Our story

We raise axolotls with the same patience and intention we’d want for our own home.

We believe every axolotl should have a calm environment, a healthy routine, and a strong start in life. That is why we focus on long-term care, gentle handling, and transparent communication from the first message to the day your new companion arrives.

Summer Axolotls Home exists for aquarium lovers who want something more than a pet — they want a well-cared-for companion with a resilient temperament and a beautifully healthy appearance.
